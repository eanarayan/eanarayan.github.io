From particle collisions to the technologies shaping academic work.

I am an experimental particle physicist currently based at Southern Methodist University in Dallas and working with the ATLAS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider at CERN.

My current physics research includes searches for rare di-Higgs production and research and development for future generations of ATLAS silicon detectors.

I received my PhD in Physics from the University of New Mexico in 2024 under the supervision of Prof. Sally Seidel. My doctoral work included measurements involving B mesons and the development and characterization of silicon sensors for particle detectors.

My broader professional interests include academic technology, artificial intelligence in higher education, scientific communication, and the ways emerging computational tools influence research and teaching.

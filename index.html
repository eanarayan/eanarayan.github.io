Who am I?

I study fundamental particles and the technologies we build to observe them.

I am an experimental particle physicist and postdoctoral researcher at Southern Methodist University in Dallas, where I collaborate with the ATLAS experiment at CERN.

My research spans particle physics, detector instrumentation, and computing. I currently work on searches for Higgs-boson pair production and on custom electronics for the upgraded ATLAS detector.

I received my Ph.D. in Experimental Particle Physics from the University of New Mexico, where my research focused on B-physics and detector development. My doctoral work included precision measurements of neutral B mesons and research on silicon particle detectors.

I have been hard of hearing since birth and use hearing aids. Captions are an important part of how I communicate, and I use them extensively during online meetings and, through my phone, during many in-person conversations as well.

Beyond particle physics, I am interested in emerging technologies, artificial intelligence, scientific communication, and how new computational tools can support research, teaching, and higher education.
